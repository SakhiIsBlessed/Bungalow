    What the World’s Leading Voices Say About Bungalow by Vikas Khanna

    Celebrated voices share their experience of Vikas Khanna’s timeless culinary journey.

    “Bungalow Proves a Celebrity Chef Can Still Draw a Crowd”

    When Vikas Khanna opened Bungalow in New York, expectations were sky-high — and they were absolutely met. The restaurant quickly became one of NYC’s toughest reservations, booked weeks in advance. The reviewer praises its strong, rooted identity: showcasing regional Indian flavors in a modern, elevated format. The menu reflects dishes from across India, including rich Lucknowi lamb chops, Bihari sattu-stuffed rotis, and a beautifully layered biryani. Each dish arrives with storytelling finesse, but not all praise is universal — the yogurt kebab was described as “too ornamental” and a few plating choices leaned into theatrics. However, the ambience, service, and Khanna’s signature hospitality sealed the deal. His star power from MasterChef India and his reputation as a humanitarian have made this more than a restaurant — it's a cultural event. The article closes by calling Bungalow a place “where food meets philosophy.”

    Source: Grub Street – March 2024

    “Bungalow Wins Michelin Bib Gourmand in 2024”

    Bungalow by Vikas Khanna proudly received the Michelin Bib Gourmand Award in 2024, joining the ranks of global culinary excellence. The honor recognizes restaurants that serve high-quality food at reasonable prices — and Bungalow was celebrated for doing just that. The Michelin team praised the restaurant's “delicate presentation” and “soulful execution” of Indian staples like seekh kebabs, dal, and poori-aloo. Chef Khanna took to social media to dedicate the award to his late sister Radhika, writing that the restaurant is “a promise kept to his family and his roots.” The recognition was not only a professional win but also a deeply emotional moment for him and his team. Bungalow stood out among more than 400 restaurants in New York, a testament to Khanna’s ability to merge nostalgia, elegance, and story-driven food in one space.

    Source: Indian Express – April 2024

    “Shah Rukh Khan Visits Bungalow, Sparks Emotional Tribute”

    In a major global moment, Amazon founder Jeff Bezos and his partner Lauren Sánchez dined at Bungalow and called the experience a “pilgrimage.” Their visit was widely covered by Indian and international media. They enjoyed a 9-course tasting menu that included black rice pudding, Bihari rotis, and sattu-stuffed kulchas — each dish curated to showcase India’s diverse culinary landscape. Bezos reportedly said that the experience felt like “a journey through India’s soul,” appreciating both the flavor and the storytelling that came with each dish. Chef Khanna personally presented some of the courses and shared his emotional backstory. Sánchez called the ambiance “dreamlike,” praising the flower-filled space and nostalgic decor. The visit was seen as a moment of validation for Khanna’s mission to bring soulful Indian cuisine into fine-dining conversations globally.

    Source: Indian Express – June 2024

    “The Office Star Jenna Fischer Dines at Bungalow, Calls It 'Magical'”

    Actress Jenna Fischer, best known for her role as Pam in The Office, visited Bungalow by Vikas Khanna and couldn’t stop raving about the experience. In a series of Instagram Stories, she described the restaurant as “magical and deeply moving.” She shared photos of her meal, which included a spice-roasted pineapple starter, rose gulkand cheesecake, and Khanna’s signature yogurt kebab. Fischer called the dishes “art on a plate” and praised the staff for their grace and hospitality. She was especially touched by Khanna’s personal greeting and his passion for preserving cultural authenticity. Her visit sparked a wave of interest among foodies and Indian diaspora communities in NYC. Media described the moment as proof of how Indian cuisine is becoming an essential part of the global fine-dining narrative.”

    Source: NDTV – April 2024

    “A tribute to tradition — Vikas Khanna’s last masterpiece.”

    In this moving feature, The Indian Express calls Bungalow “a tribute to tradition,” labeling it Vikas Khanna’s last masterpiece. The review highlights the soulful collaboration between Khanna and designer Jimmy Rizvi, who have together created a space that celebrates India’s culinary legacy with elegance. The article dives deep into how Bungalow goes beyond fine dining — it’s an immersive storytelling experience. From handcrafted woodwork to classic patterns and soft lighting, every element whispers of India’s rich heritage. The menu pays homage to regional cuisines with a modern touch, but never loses its cultural authenticity. The review describes the experience as “timeless,” and Khanna’s personal journey — from Amritsar to Michelin honors — echoes in every dish. The restaurant is seen as a cultural and emotional landmark.

    Source: The Indian Express

    “Dining here felt like coming home in a dream.” — Sonam Kapoor

    Bollywood star Sonam Kapoor shared her deeply personal experience dining at Bungalow, describing it as “coming home in a dream.” In her words, the ambiance felt warm and nostalgic — like being embraced by India while sitting in the heart of New York. She praised the staff’s heartfelt hospitality and the emotional power behind the food. Her favorite dishes were reportedly the Dal Bungalow and the Beetroot Tikki, both of which reminded her of family traditions. Kapoor emphasized how Chef Khanna’s food is rooted in memory and emotion — not just technique. She concluded her post saying she left the restaurant with a full heart, feeling like she had traveled back in time through flavors.

    Source: Sonam Kapoor

    “India’s most beautiful restaurant of 2024.”

    In a glowing feature, Vogue Lifestyle named Bungalow “India’s most beautiful restaurant of 2024.” The design, led by Shaila Rizvi, merges modern Indian elegance with the feeling of an old-world haveli. From floral ceiling canopies to carved wood, hand-painted motifs, and natural light, every inch of the restaurant is curated to create comfort and awe. The dining room is surrounded by vintage mirrors, soft blush hues, and traditional textures, making it an ideal mix of charm and chic. The article notes that the visual experience perfectly complements the rich, soul-driven food. Bungalow is not just a place to eat — it’s a place to feel, reflect, and be inspired. Vogue applauds the restaurant for redefining luxury in a way that’s personal and poetic.

    Source: Vogue Lifestyle
